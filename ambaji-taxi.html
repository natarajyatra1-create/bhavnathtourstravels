Ambaji Temple Taxi & Tour

Places to Visit in Ambaji

Ambaji Temple

The main temple of Goddess Ambaji, a Shakti Peeth near the Rajasthan border.

Gabbar Hill

A hill near Ambaji, regarded as the original seat of the Goddess, with a ropeway.

Kumbhariya Jain Temples

A group of ancient marble Jain temples near Ambaji.

Mansarovar Kund

A holy tank near the main temple.

Balaram Palace, Palanpur

A palace and heritage hotel near Palanpur, on the way.

Bhadarvi Poonam Fair

A large fair of pilgrims held in the month of Bhadarvo.

Suggested Plan

  1. 1Early morning drive towards Ambaji.

Best Time & Vehicles

Best time: October to March. During the Bhadarvi Poonam fair the town is crowded, so book vehicles early.

Vehicles: Hatchback, Sedan, Premium Sedan, Ertiga, Innova Crysta, Luxury SUV, Tempo Traveller and Bus. We suggest the right vehicle for your group size.

Services: One-way drop, round trip, airport and railway station transfer, door to door pickup, corporate travel and group booking.
