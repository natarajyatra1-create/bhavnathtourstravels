Frequently Asked Questions

            

            

                

                    Which places in Gujarat do you cover for taxi and tour packages?+
                    
We run taxi, cab and tour packages across all of Gujarat, including Junagadh, Girnar, Somnath, Dwarka, Bet Dwarka, Sasan Gir, Diu, Porbandar, Rajkot, Jamnagar, Bhavnagar, Palitana, Kutch, Ahmedabad, Gandhinagar, Statue of Unity, Saputara and more.

                

                

                    Do you provide one-way drop and round-trip taxi?+
                    
Yes. We offer one-way drops and round trips from Junagadh and Bhavnath to any city in Gujarat, with door to door pickup and drop.

                

                

                    Can I book a taxi from Rajkot Airport or Ahmedabad Airport to Junagadh?+
                    
Yes. Airport and railway station transfers to Junagadh, Somnath, Dwarka and Sasan Gir are available. Share your flight or train time and we will arrange the pickup.

                

                

                    Which vehicles are available?+
                    
Hatchbacks, sedans, premium sedans, SUVs such as Ertiga and Innova Crysta, luxury SUVs, Tempo Travellers and buses for groups. Tell us the number of passengers and we will suggest the right vehicle.

                

                

                    Do you offer corporate travel and group bookings?+
                    
Yes. We provide corporate travel, family trips, yatra groups, weddings and school trips with Tempo Travellers and buses.
