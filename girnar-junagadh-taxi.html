Places to Visit in Junagadh & Girnar

Girnar Hill

The sacred hill with about 10,000 stone steps leading to the Dattatreya peak, with Jain and Hindu temples on the way. Most devotees start before sunrise.

Girnar Ropeway

A ropeway runs from Girnar Taleti up to the Ambaji temple, saving the long climb.

Bhavnath Mahadev Temple

The ancient Shiva temple at the foot of Girnar, known for the Bhavnath fair held on Mahashivratri.

Uparkot Fort

An ancient hill fort in the middle of Junagadh with Buddhist caves, old step wells and the Adi Kadi Vav.

Ashoka Rock Edicts

Inscriptions of the Mauryan emperor Ashoka carved on a large boulder near Girnar.

Mahabat Maqbara

A mausoleum of the Junagadh nawabs, known for its spiral minarets and Indo-Islamic design.

Damodar Kund

A sacred kund at the base of Girnar, linked with the Damodarji temple.

Sakkarbaug Zoo

A well known zoo in Junagadh, famous for its Asiatic lions.
