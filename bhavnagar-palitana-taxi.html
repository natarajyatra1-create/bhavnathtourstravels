Places to Visit in Bhavnagar & Palitana

Palitana Shatrunjaya

A Jain pilgrimage hill with over 3,000 steps and hundreds of marble temples.

Velavadar Blackbuck National Park

A grassland park famous for blackbuck, wolves and harriers.

Gopnath Beach

A quiet beach near Talaja with a Shiva temple on the cliff.

Takhteshwar Temple

A hill temple in Bhavnagar with a view of the city.

Nilambag Palace

A palace of the former rulers of Bhavnagar, now a heritage hotel.

Gaurishankar Lake

The Bor Talav lake in the city.

Talaja Caves

Ancient Buddhist rock caves near Talaja.

Best Time & Vehicles

Best time: October to March. Shatrunjaya is closed during the monsoon months for the climb.

Vehicles: Hatchback, Sedan, Premium Sedan, Ertiga, Innova Crysta, Luxury SUV, Tempo Traveller and Bus. We suggest the right vehicle for your group size.

Services: One-way drop, round trip, airport and railway station transfer, door to door pickup, corporate travel and group booking.
