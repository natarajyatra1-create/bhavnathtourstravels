Statue of Unity Taxi & Tour

The Statue of Unity at Kevadia, now Ekta Nagar, is the tallest statue in the world. We arrange taxis and group vehicles from Junagadh, Rajkot, Ahmedabad and Vadodara for the Statue of Unity and nearby attractions.

About 450 km from Junagadh by road via Vadodara (approx.) · about 8 hours

Places to Visit in Statue of Unity

Statue of Unity

A 182 metre statue of Sardar Vallabhbhai Patel, the tallest statue in the world.

Viewing Gallery

A viewing gallery high in the statue with views of the Sardar Sarovar Dam and the hills.

Sardar Sarovar Dam

A major dam on the Narmada river near the statue.

Valley of Flowers

A garden near the statue with seasonal blooms.

Jungle Safari

A safari park near Ekta Nagar with animals and birds.

Ekta Nagar Museum and Gardens

Exhibitions, gardens and a children's park at the site.

Suggested Plan

  1. 1Early morning start from Vadodara or Ahmedabad is ideal.
  2. 2Statue of Unity and viewing gallery.
  3. 3Valley of Flowers and dam view, return in the evening.

Best Time & Vehicles

Vehicles: Hatchback, Sedan, Premium Sedan, Ertiga, Innova Crysta, Luxury SUV, Tempo Traveller and Bus. We suggest the right vehicle for your group size.

Services: One-way drop, round trip, airport and railway station transfer, door to door pickup, corporate travel and group booking.
