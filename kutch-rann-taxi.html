Kutch & White Rann Taxi and Tour

Kutch is Gujarat's largest district, famous for the white salt desert of the Rann. We arrange taxis and tempo travellers from Junagadh and Rajkot to Bhuj and the Rann, for Rann Utsav and for sightseeing.

Places to Visit in Kutch & Rann

White Rann of Kutch, Dhordo

A huge white salt desert that looks like snow in the moonlight. Rann Utsav is usually held in winter.

Kala Dungar

The Black Hill, one of the highest points in Kutch, with a wide view of the Rann.

Bhuj: Aina Mahal and Prag Mahal

Two palaces in old Bhuj with mirrors, carvings and a clock tower view.

Hamirsar Lake

A lake in the middle of Bhuj city.

Mandvi Beach and Vijay Vilas Palace

A long beach and a royal summer palace in Mandvi.

Dholavira

An ancient Harappan city, a UNESCO World Heritage Site.

Mata no Madh

A famous temple of Ashapura Mata.

Narayan Sarovar and Koteshwar

Holy places near the western end of Kutch.

Bhujodi Village

A craft village known for weaving and embroidery.

Suggested Plan

  1. 1Day 1: Junagadh or Rajkot to Bhuj, Bhuj city sights.
  2. 2Day 2: Kala Dungar and the White Rann at sunset.
  3. 3Day 3: Mandvi and Dholavira on request, or return.
  4. 4A night stay at the Rann is popular during Rann Utsav.
