Saputara Hill Station Taxi & Tour

Saputara is Gujarat's popular hill station in the Dang district. We arrange taxis from Surat, Vadodara, Ahmedabad and Rajkot to Saputara and nearby Dang attractions.

About 650 km from Junagadh by road (approx.), much closer from Surat · about 11 to 12 hours from Junagadh

Places to Visit in Saputara

Saputara Lake

A lake with boating in the middle of the hill town.

Sunset Point

A point with wide valley views, popular in the evening.

Table Point and Echo Point

Flat top hills with views of the Sahyadri range.

Gira Falls

A waterfall near Waghai, best in and after the monsoon.

Vansda National Park

A forest park with leopards, deer and many birds.

Dang Darbar, Ahwa

A tribal fair held around Holi in Ahwa.

Suggested Plan

  1. 1Pickup from Surat or Vadodara and drive to Saputara.
  2. 2Lake, Sunset Point and Table Point.
  3. 3Gira Falls or Vansda National Park, then return.

Best Time & Vehicles

Best time: July to September for greenery and waterfalls, and winter for pleasant weather.

Vehicles: Hatchback, Sedan, Premium Sedan, Ertiga, Innova Crysta, Luxury SUV, Tempo Traveller and Bus. We suggest the right vehicle for your group size.

Services: One-way drop, round trip, airport and railway station transfer, door to door pickup, corporate travel and group booking.

Frequently Asked Questions

How far is Saputara from Surat?+

About 165 km, roughly 4 hours.

Is Saputara good in the monsoon?+

Yes, the hills are green and waterfalls are full, but roads can be slippery. Our drivers are used to ghat roads.

Do you arrange tempo traveller for groups?+

Yes, Tempo Travellers are available for groups and families.
