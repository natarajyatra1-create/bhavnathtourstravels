Junagadh to Sasan Gir Taxi & Lion Safari Trip

Gir National Park is the only place in the wild where Asiatic lions live. We take you from Junagadh, Somnath or Rajkot to Sasan Gir, wait for the safari time and arrange the return.

About 55 to 60 km from Junagadh by road (approx.) · about 1.5 hours

Places to Visit in Sasan Gir

Gir Jungle Safari

Jeep safari in the Gir forest to see Asiatic lions, leopards and many birds. Permits are booked through the Gujarat forest department, so book in advance.

Devalia Safari Park

Also called the Gir Interpretation Zone, a shorter and easier safari near Sasan.

Kankai Mata Temple

A temple deep inside the Gir forest, visited by devotees.

Tulsishyam Temple

A temple on a hill in the Gir forest with hot water springs nearby.

Kamleshwar Dam

The large Hiran dam near Sasan, known for birds and crocodiles.

Suggested Plan

  1. 1Pickup from Junagadh early morning to reach Sasan Gir before your safari slot.
  2. 2Jungle safari or Devalia Safari Park.
  3. 3Lunch, optional Kankai Mata or Tulsishyam visit, and return, or continue to Somnath.

Best Time & Vehicles

Best time: The park is open from 16 October to 15 June and closed in the monsoon. Winter mornings are the most popular.

Vehicles: Hatchback, Sedan, Premium Sedan, Ertiga, Innova Crysta, Luxury SUV, Tempo Traveller and Bus. We suggest the right vehicle for your group size.

Services: One-way drop, round trip, airport and railway station transfer, door to door pickup, corporate travel and group booking.

Frequently Asked Questions

When is Gir safari closed?+

The Gir forest safari is closed from 16 June to 15 October for the monsoon, and Devalia may follow similar schedules. Check the current notice before travel.

Do you book the safari permit?+

Permits are booked online through the Gujarat forest department. We can guide you on the process and plan the taxi around your slot.

Can I visit Sasan Gir and Somnath together?+

Yes. They are close, so a 2 day Gir and Somnath trip is a popular package.
